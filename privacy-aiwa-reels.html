Privacy Policy

Aiwa Reels — internal publishing tool for Facebook
Developer: Aiwa Int · Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com
Last updated: 2 November 2026

Aiwa Reels is a private tool that Aiwa Int uses to publish short videos about its own games to its own Facebook Page. It has no public sign-up, and nobody else logs in to it.

1. Data used

  • Page access. The tool uses an access token for the Aiwa Int Facebook Page, and the Page's ID and name, to publish videos and check their processing status. These are stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • Published content. The videos and captions are created by Aiwa Int and sent to Meta for publication.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data about people who view, like or comment on the videos.
  • We do not sell or share data obtained through the tool, or use it for advertising.

3. Data deletion

The tool holds no data about other people, so there is nothing of theirs to delete. To ask a question or make a request, email aiwainit2023@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

4. Meta

Videos published on Facebook are also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
